# Porter Operations Dashboard

A business intelligence and operational analytics dashboard designed to monitor KPIs, operational performance, reporting metrics, and business insights.

---

## Overview

This project focuses on building operational dashboards and reporting systems for tracking business performance and operational efficiency.

The dashboard enables data-driven monitoring of KPIs, operational trends, and performance metrics to support business decision-making.

---

## Key Features

- KPI monitoring dashboards
- Operational performance tracking
- Business reporting workflows
- Trend analysis & insights
- Data visualization
- Automated reporting support
- Performance analytics

---
